# Roadmap

## North star

> Proof of authorship and provable engineering for the RoboPay Unitree Go2
> Tier-1 submission: this repo's git history and release tags are the
> evidence, and the committed physics reports are the measurements.

## Sequenced next

1. **Keep the archive honest** — this repo is the original-authorship
   archive for the Go2 Tier-1 submission; any changes stay scoped to the 9
   skills (including obstacle navigation) and their measured results.
2. **Reproducibility** — document the exact steps (menagerie `unitree_go2`
   model + x402 payment gate + optional Base Sepolia EIP-3009 settlement) so
   anyone can re-run a paid skill episode or the live settlement proof.
3. **Distribution** — link the submission from the RoboPay bounty and from
   the flagship robot-sim repo, so the work is discoverable.

## Explicitly out of scope (for now)

- Expanding beyond the simulator scope (the profile is explicitly
  `simulator-only`).
- Forking or renaming the archive.
- Marketing copy that isn't backed by the measured skill table or the
  committed on-chain settlement evidence.
